Warranty & Subscription Terms

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Service Scope

RAKEXURA provides private profile slot access and subscription management for Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and NVIDIA GeForce NOW. All subscriptions are calibrated to the exact timestamp of checkout.

2. Durations & Precision Timing

Subscriptions are available in 1, 2, 3, 6, and 12-month validity tiers. Active timing countdown starts immediately upon credential delivery.

3. 100% Lifetime Replacement Warranty

Every active subscription includes full warranty coverage. In the unlikely event of an authentication issue or login lock, a replacement slot is provisioned at no additional cost.

4. Private PIN Security

Customers are assigned dedicated profile slots with individual PIN protection. Sharing credentials outside authorized devices is prohibited.
